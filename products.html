Products

The released datasets on each domain, with their versions

Each dataset is released on the domains below, one package per domain, dataset and grid on Helmholtz Codebase. The files of each grid are listed at the end of the page of each dataset, under Downloads; download them with fesmdata.jl (see Download) or one by one. What each dataset contains is under Datasets, the domains and their grids under Domains and grids.
