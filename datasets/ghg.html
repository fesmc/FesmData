GHG: greenhouse gases from ice cores

Atmospheric CO2, CH4 and N2O over glacial cycles, from Antarctic ice cores: the records of EPICA Dome C, a composite of several cores, and spline-smoothed series with their radiative forcing. GHG is a records dataset (non-gridded data, see Records): its products, defined in GHG/records.toml, are prepared files in the form of the CF discrete sampling geometries, written to $FESMDATA/Records/GHG/GHG-<product>.nc, each with its text form (.txt).

Products

Product Source Site or region Variables Time
GHG-Bereiter2015 Bereiter et al. (2015): revised Antarctic CO2 composite, doi:10.25921/n8y4-bp27 Antarctic ice cores (composite) co2, co2_sd (ppm) 0-806 ka, 1901 values (gas ages, mostly AICC2012)
GHG-Loulergue2008 Loulergue et al. (2008): EPICA Dome C methane, doi:10.25921/gfsj-jc86 EDC ch4, ch4_sd (ppb), lab, depth 0-799 ka, 2103 values (EDC3 gas age; also AICC2023)
GHG-Schilt2010 Schilt et al. (2010): EPICA Dome C nitrous oxide, doi:10.1016/j.quascirev.2009.03.011 EDC n2o, n2o_artifact, n2o_sd (ppb), depth 0-798 ka, 912 values (EDC3 gas age; also AICC2023)
GHG-Koehler2017 Köhler et al. (2017): spline-smoothed CO2, CH4 and N2O and their radiative forcing, doi:10.1594/PANGAEA.871273 global co2 (ppm), ch4, n2o (ppb), rf_co2, rf_ch4, rf_n2o (W m-2), each with _sd 2016 CE to 156 ka (N2O to 135 ka), every year

The records of EDC are time series at the site (featureType timeSeries, dimension station); the composite and the splines are series of a region without a location (dimension region, region_name, lat and lon missing). time is in years before 1950 CE (units “years ago”), the gas age on the time scale of the source (in the comment of time). The records of EDC, with depths, also have time_aicc2023, the gas age on AICC2023 (Bouchet et al., 2023) interpolated linearly in depth in the AICC2023 table of EDC (see IceCores). The composite of Bereiter et al. (2015) has no depths and is kept on its published ages. Uncertainties _sd are 1 sigma.

Making the dataset

julia fesmdata.jl record GHG

prepares the missing sources (<Source>/prepare.jl), checks the prepared files and copies them to $FESMDATA/Records/GHG/ with their text forms (--overwrite to replace them).
