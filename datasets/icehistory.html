IceHistory: paleo ice sheets and topography

Reconstructions of past ice sheets and topography (glacial cycles, Pliocene), remapped onto the grids of the domains and onto global lon-lat grids. IceHistory is a thematic dataset made by remapping (see Gridded data): its products are defined in IceHistory/remap.toml.

Each file <GRID>_IceHistory-<Source>.nc also has f_valid, the fraction of each cell covered by the source. Fields are remapped conservatively and, on grids finer than the source, smoothed with a Gaussian of half the source spacing; the masks are remapped as classes (the class covering most of each cell). Ice extents from outlines (Batchelor, Margold) are rasterized as area fractions at 2 km and 1 km, so that remapping gives the fraction of each cell. The global attribute remap_method records what was done on each grid.

Precision

The float fields are rounded on output to a tolerance by kind (see Precision and file sizes): elevations and thicknesses (z_*, dz_*, H_ice*) to 0.01 m; fractions (f_*) to 0.001; ages (age_*) to 3 significant digits. The masks of PRISM4 are exact.

Making the dataset

julia -t 8 fesmdata.jl remap IceHistory

See GHF for making single products and adding a source.
