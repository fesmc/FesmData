SMB: surface mass balance and surface climate

Surface mass balance and surface climate of the ice sheets from regional climate models, remapped onto the grids of the domains. SMB is a thematic dataset made by remapping (see Gridded data): its products are defined in SMB/remap.toml.

All sources use the same names and units: fluxes in kg m-2 yr-1 (mm water equivalent per year), also for the months of a climatology, and temperatures in K. The -annual-mean products are the annual means (x, y) of the monthly climatologies, the months weighted by their mean length over the period. Each file also has f_valid, the fraction of each cell covered by the source. Fields are remapped conservatively and smoothed on grids finer than the source; the masks are remapped as classes.

SMB-RACMO2.3p2-1985-2014 (and its annual mean) is a reference climate for model forcing (the reference period of the ISMIP7 experiments), defined on the whole grid: smb is pr + subl off the glaciated surfaces (see vanWessem2023_racmo23), and smb, pr, sf, t2m, T_srf and z_srf are filled outside the RACMO domain (the corners of the grids) by harmonic interpolation (fill, mask = "all", marked by mask_fill).

SMB-RACMO2.3p2-1981-2010, SMB-MARv3.14-1961-1990 and SMB-MARv3.14-1981-2010 (and their annual means) are also defined on the whole grid, for model forcing, but filled where there are no source data (f_valid = 0, marked by mask_fill_<field>) as follows:

  • smb is defined on the ice only (RACMO: f_ice >= 0.1; MAR: its ice mask, see Fettweis2017_mar314, so that the cells at the ice margin are not mixed with the strongly negative values of MAR off the ice). Off the ice and outside the RCM domain (e.g. the ice caps of the Canadian Arctic on the GRL grids), it is the linear regression of smb on the surface elevation over the cells with data within 150 km (grown until there are 20), at the elevation of the cell (max(z_srf, 0) of TOPO-BedMachine-v4) clamped to the range of the elevations of these cells (no extrapolation), at most -91.7 kg m-2 yr-1 (-0.1 m yr-1 ice equivalent, with 917 kg m-3): a small negative surface mass balance off the ice, on land and ocean. In the monthly climatologies the bound applies to the annual mean (months weighted by their length), all months shifted by the same amount (fill, method = "elevation_regression", see Gap filling).
  • t2m and T_srf: the same regression on the elevation (a local lapse rate), without bound. They are missing only outside the RCM domain (RACMO: the corners of the grids; MAR: e.g. the Canadian Arctic), as the RCMs give them on land and ocean.
  • z_srf (the RCM orography): the elevation of the regression, max(z_srf, 0) of TOPO-BedMachine-v4, in every cell where smb is filled (method = "elevation", gaps_of = "smb"), also where the RCM has an orography of its own (e.g. 0 over the ocean of MAR, in the Canadian Arctic), so that it is the reference elevation of the filled smb (e.g. for an elevation correction in a model). Elsewhere it is the RCM orography, the elevation of the RCM t2m and T_srf where only smb is filled.
  • pr, sf (and rf for MAR, so that pr = sf + rf holds): harmonic interpolation. Off the ice, smb is not pr minus runoff or sublimation (which stay missing there), but the bound above.

runoff, melt and subl are not filled.

Precision

The float fields are rounded on output to a tolerance by kind (see Precision and file sizes): smb, pr, sf, rf, runoff, melt and subl to 1%; t2m and T_srf to 0.01 K; z_srf to 0.01 m; f_ice and f_valid to 0.001. The masks are exact.
