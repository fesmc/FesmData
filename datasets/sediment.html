Sediment: sediment thickness and properties

Sediment thickness (m) from global and regional maps, and sediment properties of North America and Greenland, remapped onto the grids of the domains and onto global lon-lat grids. Sediment is a thematic dataset made by remapping (see Gridded data): its products are defined in Sediment/remap.toml.

Products

Product Source Fields Domains
Sediment-CRUST1 Laske et al. (2013): CRUST1.0, 1° global crustal model, land and ocean (igppweb.ucsd.edu/~gabi/crust1.html); its sediments are those of Laske and Masters (1997), the global sediment map of the v1 product SED-L97, which it replaces z_sed (total), z_sed_upper, z_sed_middle, z_sed_lower (layers) Antarctica, GreenlandPaleo, Greenland, North, Laurentide, Eurasia, Global
Sediment-GlobSed Straume et al. (2019): GlobSed v3, 5’ total sediment thickness of the oceans (missing on land), doi:10.1029/2018GC008115 z_sed Antarctica, GreenlandPaleo, Greenland, North, Laurentide, Eurasia, Global
Sediment-ECM1 Mooney et al. (2023): ECM1, 1° global crustal model, land and ocean, doi:10.1016/j.earscirev.2023.104493; the v1 product SED-M23 z_sed (total), z_sed_upper, z_sed_middle, z_sed_lower (layers) Antarctica, GreenlandPaleo, Greenland, North, Laurentide, Eurasia, Global
Sediment-ANTASed Baranov et al. (2021): ANTASed, 0.1° (11 km) map of the Antarctic continent (grounded ice and ice shelves, missing in the ocean), doi:10.3389/feart.2021.722699 z_sed Antarctica
Sediment-Gowan2019 Gowan et al. (2019): 5 km maps of the surface sediments of North America, Greenland and Iceland (areas of the former ice sheets; missing elsewhere), doi:10.1594/PANGAEA.895889; the v1 product SED-G19 grain (1 clay, 2 sand, 3 silt), distribution (1 blanket, 2 veneer, 3 rock), composite (0-8, both combined); classes GreenlandPaleo, Greenland, Laurentide

On the mountain-glacier domains (Pyrenees, Patagonia, Himalaya), on their 2 km grid: Sediment-CRUST1 and Sediment-ECM1, and Sediment-GlobSed on Patagonia (ocean only); their nests and crops get these by cropping (see Nests and crops).

Each file <GRID>_Sediment-<Source>.nc also has f_valid, the fraction of each cell covered by the source. Each source is remapped conservatively; on grids finer than the source it is then smoothed with a Gaussian of half the source spacing. CRUST1.0 contains the sediment map of Laske and Masters (1997) (A Global Digital Map of Sediment Thickness, EOS Trans. AGU, 78, F483; igppweb.ucsd.edu/~gabi/sediment.html) unchanged: on its 1° grid, the total thickness and the three layers agree with the original files (sedmap.map, map1.thk1-3) to within 0.01 km, the rounding of the stored values. The classes of Gowan et al. (2019) are remapped as classes: each cell gets the class that covers most of it. The global attribute remap_method records what was done on each grid.

Precision

The float fields are rounded on output to a tolerance by kind (see Precision and file sizes): the thicknesses (z_sed*) to 0.01 m; f_valid to 0.001. The classes of Gowan2019 are exact.

Making the dataset

julia -t 8 fesmdata.jl remap Sediment

See GHF for making single products and adding a source.
