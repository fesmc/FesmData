Atmosphere: reanalysis and satellite climatologies

Monthly climatologies of the atmosphere from reanalysis and satellite data, remapped onto the grids of the domains and onto global lon-lat grids. Atmosphere is a thematic dataset made by remapping (see Gridded data): its products are defined in Atmosphere/remap.toml.

Products

Product Source Fields Domains
Atmosphere-ERA5-1981-2010, Atmosphere-ERA5-1991-2020 Hersbach et al. (2020): ERA5 monthly means, N320 Gaussian grid (about 31 km), doi:10.1002/qj.3803 zs, lsm; monthly sp, t2m, sst, u10, v10, ws10, tcc, al, tcw, tclw, tciw, pr, sf Antarctica, GreenlandPaleo, Greenland, North, Laurentide, Eurasia, Global
Atmosphere-ERA5-plev-1981-2010, Atmosphere-ERA5-plev-1991-2020 as above, 9 pressure levels from 1000 to 500 hPa t, z, u, v, w, uv on plev, monthly Antarctica, GreenlandPaleo, Greenland, North, Laurentide, Eurasia, Global
Atmosphere-CERES-2001-2020 Loeb et al. (2018): CERES EBAF Ed4.2.1, 1°, doi:10.1175/JCLI-D-17-0208.1 TOA and surface radiative fluxes (W m-2), monthly Antarctica, GreenlandPaleo, Greenland, North, Laurentide, Eurasia, Global

On the mountain-glacier domains (Pyrenees, Patagonia, Himalaya), on their 2 km grid: Atmosphere-ERA5-1981-2010 and Atmosphere-ERA5-1991-2020; their nests and crops get these by cropping (see Nests and crops).

Winds are remapped as vectors and rotated to the axes of projected grids. Each file also has f_valid, the fraction of each cell covered by the source. Fields are remapped conservatively and smoothed on grids finer than the source.

Precision

The float fields are rounded on output to a tolerance by kind (see Precision and file sizes): temperatures (t2m, sst, t) to 0.01 K; the elevation zs and the geopotential height z to 0.01 m; fractions (lsm, tcc, al, f_valid) to 0.001; precipitation, snowfall, winds, w and the radiative fluxes to 1%; sp, tcw, tclw and tciw to 3 significant digits.

Making the dataset

julia -t 8 fesmdata.jl remap Atmosphere
