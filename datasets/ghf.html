GHF: geothermal heat flow

Geothermal heat flow (mW m-2) from published maps, remapped onto the grids of the domains and onto global lon-lat grids. GHF is a thematic dataset made by remapping (see Gridded data): its products are defined in GHF/remap.toml.

Products

Product Source Fields Domains
GHF-Lucazeau2019 Lucazeau (2019): 0.5° global map fitted to heat flow observations, doi:10.1029/2019GC008389 ghf, ghf_sd (standard deviation) Antarctica, GreenlandPaleo, Greenland, North, Laurentide, Eurasia, Global
GHF-Martos2017 Martos et al. (2017): 15 km map of Antarctica from airborne magnetic data (Curie depth), doi:10.1594/PANGAEA.882503 ghf, ghf_sd (uncertainty), depth_curie, depth_curie_sd (km) Antarctica
GHF-Martos2018 Martos et al. (2018): 15 km map of Greenland (land only) from magnetic Curie depths, doi:10.1594/PANGAEA.892973 ghf, ghf_sd (uncertainty), depth_curie, depth_curie_sd (km) Greenland
GHF-HazzardRichards2024 Hazzard and Richards (2024): 0.5° map of Antarctica inferred from seismic velocities, doi:10.1029/2023GL106274 ghf, ghf_sd (standard deviation) Antarctica
GHF-Davies2013 Davies (2013): 2° global map from observations, geology and young-ocean cooling, doi:10.1002/ggge.20271 ghf (mean), ghf_median, ghf_err (error estimate) Antarctica, GreenlandPaleo, Greenland, North, Laurentide, Eurasia, Global
GHF-Shapiro2004 Shapiro and Ritzwoller (2004): 1° global map extrapolated with a seismic model, doi:10.1016/j.epsl.2004.04.011 ghf, ghf_sd (standard deviation) Antarctica, GreenlandPaleo, Greenland, North, Laurentide, Eurasia, Global
GHF-Loesing2021 Lösing and Ebbing (2021): machine-learning map of Antarctica, about 55 km, doi:10.1594/PANGAEA.930237 ghf, ghf_min, ghf_max (range of alternative models) Antarctica
GHF-Stal2020 Stål et al. (2021): Aq1, 20 km map of Antarctica, doi:10.1594/PANGAEA.924857 ghf, ghf_unc (uncertainty) Antarctica
GHF-Colgan2021 Colgan and Wansing (2021): machine-learning map of Greenland, about 55 km, doi:10.22008/FK2/F9P03L ghf, ghf_min, ghf_max (ensemble range) Greenland
GHF-FoxMaule2005 Fox Maule et al. (2005): map from satellite magnetic data (Curie depth), updated with the MF7 model, about 170 km, land only, doi:10.1126/science.1106888 ghf Antarctica (no data south of 87.75°S), GreenlandPaleo, Greenland
GHF-Colgan2021-topocorr-GRL Colgan et al. (2021): relative topographic correction at 150 m (BedMachine), doi:10.22008/FK2/BQGYYG ghf_corr, ghf_corr_unc (dimensionless) Greenland
GHF-Colgan2021-topocorr-ANT Colgan et al. (2021): relative topographic correction at 500 m (BedMachine), doi:10.22008/FK2/BQGYYG ghf_corr, ghf_corr_unc (dimensionless) Antarctica

On the mountain-glacier domains (Pyrenees, Patagonia, Himalaya), on their 2 km grid: GHF-Lucazeau2019, GHF-Davies2013 and GHF-Shapiro2004; their nests and crops get these by cropping (see Nests and crops).

The topographic correction of Colgan et al. (2021) is not a heat flow but a dimensionless relative correction (about 0 on average), to be applied to any heat flow product as corrected heat flow = (1 + ghf_corr) × ghf (higher heat flow in valleys, lower on ridges). It is made on all grids, as it resolves the bed topography finer than any of them.

Each file <GRID>_GHF-<Source>.nc also has f_valid, the fraction of each cell covered by the source. Each source is remapped conservatively; on grids finer than the source it is then smoothed with a Gaussian of half the source spacing (about 28 km for 0.5°), so that its cells do not show as steps. The global attribute remap_method records what was done on each grid.

GHF-Martos2018 covers the land of Greenland only: ghf and ghf_sd are filled where there are no source data (cells at the ice margin, the ocean and the rest of the grid) by harmonic interpolation (fill, mask = "all", marked by mask_fill; see Gap filling), so that they cover the whole grid. depth_curie and depth_curie_sd are not filled.

Precision

The float fields are rounded on output to a tolerance by kind (see Precision and file sizes): the heat flow and its uncertainties and ranges (mW m-2) to 1%; depth_curie and depth_curie_sd (km) to 3 significant digits; ghf_corr, ghf_corr_unc (dimensionless) and f_valid to 0.001.

Making the dataset

julia -t 8 fesmdata.jl remap GHF
# on Levante, on a compute node:
# sbatch --job-name=remap-GHF Remap/jobs/remap_dataset.sbatch GHF

This makes all products on all their grids, after preparing the sources that are not prepared yet (<source>/prepare.jl). To make one product, or some domains or grids, name them, e.g. julia fesmdata.jl remap GHF Lucazeau2019 Antarctica GLOBAL-0.5DEG. Existing files are kept unless --overwrite.

Adding a source

  1. Make a folder for the source with a prepare.jl and a README.md (see Sources), e.g. Lucazeau2019_ghf/.
  2. Add a table [products.<Name>] to GHF/remap.toml with source, file, variables and grids (and method or smooth if the defaults do not fit).
  3. Add it to the table of products above, and run julia fesmdata.jl remap GHF <Name>.
  4. Release a new version of GHF (see Releasing).

Download the files with fesmdata.jl into $FESMDATA_PRODUCTS/<Domain>/<GRID>/ (see Download), or one by one from the links below. All released datasets are listed under Products.
