Paleoclimate: simulations and reconstructions of past climates

Climatologies of past climates from the PMIP model intercomparisons, CLIMBER-3α and CLIMBER-X (stadial and interstadial climates, with the transient output of their simulation), and gridded reconstructions of the deglacial climate of Greenland, remapped onto the grids of the domains. Paleoclimate is a thematic dataset made by remapping (see Gridded data): its products are defined in Paleoclimate/remap.toml.

On the mountain-glacier domains (Pyrenees, Patagonia, Himalaya), on their 2 km grid: the ensemble means and standard deviations of PMIP3 and PMIP4 (-mean, -stdev; not the members); their nests and crops get these by cropping (see Nests and crops).

The model output is on the models’ own grids (some Gaussian), remapped conservatively. Products are made on grids of 8 km and coarser (16 km for the Buizert et al. time series, 32 km for CLIMBER-3α and CLIMBER-X, which are also on global grids of 1° and coarser): finer grids add no information to these sources, and models can remap them online. Each file also has f_valid, the fraction of each cell covered by the source. The ensemble products are made from the files of the models on each grid: in each cell, the mean or the standard deviation (population) of the models with a value there (e.g. sst_ann only over the ocean of each model), n_<field> models.

Precision

The float fields are rounded on output to a tolerance by kind (see Precision and file sizes): temperatures to 0.01 K; precipitation to 1%; z_srf and dz_srf to 0.01 m; fractions (f_*, pr_frac*) to 0.001. The ensemble products are computed from the rounded products of their members and rounded again.
