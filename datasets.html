Datasets

What is released, by topic

Each dataset gathers products on one theme, e.g. the geothermal heat flow maps of several sources (GHF), on the grids of the domains where they apply. Topo and Regions are made by their own pipelines on every domain, from the base grid to the coarsest grid; the other gridded datasets are made by remapping published maps, on the grids that their sources resolve (see Precision and file sizes). The Records are non-gridded data (time series, sites, profiles). The page of each dataset describes its products, their sources and licences, and lists its files under Downloads; Products shows which dataset is on which domain.
